# Perils & Exclusions Schedule — CAT Events (Demo Corpus)

Companion schedule to the Meridian Mutual Homeowner Policy. Synthetic demo
document for the INS-04 portal.

## Covered catastrophe perils and their document requirements

| Peril | Covered? | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Cyclone wind damage (roof, siding, windows) | Yes | Photos of damage, weather service advisory reference |
| Storm surge / flood ingress (with Flood & Surge Cover INCLUDED) | Yes | Water line photos, Flood & Surge Cover endorsement in schedule |
| Storm surge / flood ingress (Flood & Surge Cover NOT in schedule) | No — advise referral | Flood & Surge Cover endorsement |
| Wildfire | Yes | Fire authority incident number or evacuation notice |
| Earth movement / landslip | No — excluded (wording §2e) | Not required; refuse with citation |
| Landscaping / garden loss | Only up to $2,500 aggregate | Before/after photos |

## Hardship signals that trigger the fast-path (wording §5)

- Occupant elderly (70+) or disabled
- Medical device dependency (oxygen, dialysis, refrigerated medication)
- Children under 6 in the household
- Displaced from home (uninhabitable property)
- Loss of power exceeding 72 hours
- Primary livelihood destroyed (home business, work tools)

## Escalation rules for triage

1. Any structural damage → inspection required before settlement.
2. Any water ingress → moisture reading within 7 days to prevent mould; note
   this is a health risk for households with children or medical dependency.
3. Displaced households → ALE advance within 48 hours if fast-path applies.

## Evidence states

- **available**: damage photos and a required-evidence item are present.
- **partial**: policy reference present but no damage photos.
- **missing**: no policy reference and no damage photos — request documents
  before assessment.
