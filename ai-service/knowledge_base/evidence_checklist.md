# Evidence & Document Checklist — Claims Intake (Demo Corpus)

What policyholders should attach and what adjusters request first. Synthetic
demo document for the INS-04 portal.

## Photo evidence standards

- Wide shot of the whole affected area, then close-ups of each damage point.
- Water claims: a photo of the highest visible water line with a ruler or
  tape in frame.
- Wind claims: photos of roof and siding from ground level; do not climb.
- Timestamped phone photos are accepted; EXIF is not required.

## Documents

- Policy schedule page (shows which endorsements are INCLUDED).
- Inventory of damaged contents over $5,000 (wording §6c).
- Receipts for emergency mitigation (tarp, pump hire) — reimbursable.
- ALE receipts for hotel and meals (wording §4).
- Signed hardship declaration form for the vulnerability fast-path.

## Common mistakes

- Photos of the same damage point from one angle only — request a second angle.
- Estimates for repairs attached before inspection — hold in file, do not
  treat as evidence of cost.
- Claims reported after 30 days (wording §6a) — flag to the manager, do not
  auto-reject.
