# Meridian Mutual Homeowner Policy — Wording (Demo Corpus)

Policy year 2026. This is a synthetic demo document for the INS-04 CAT Claims
Surge Portal. It is the primary grounding source for coverage checks.

## 1. Insured perils

We insure the dwelling, detached structures and contents named in the schedule
against DIRECT physical loss caused by:

1. **Windstorm and cyclone** — including damage caused by wind-driven rain that
   enters through an opening created by the same windstorm event.
2. **Flood and storm surge** — only where Flood & Surge Cover is shown as
   INCLUDED in the schedule. Water must have entered at or above ground level
   from an external source.
3. **Fire and wildfire** — including smoke damage within 72 hours of the fire.
4. **Falling trees and impact** — when caused by an insured peril.

## 2. Standard exclusions

The following are NOT covered under any section:

a. Loss caused by gradual deterioration, wear and tear or maintenance failure.
b. Damage to landscaping, shrubs and trees beyond $2,500 in the aggregate.
c. Loss arising while the property was unoccupied for more than 60 consecutive
   days without our written consent.
d. Water that entered through pre-existing openings, defective roofing or
   unsealed penetrations that existed before the catastrophe event.
e. Earth movement of any kind, including subsidence and landslip.

## 3. Deductibles

- Standard deductible: $1,000 per claim.
- **Named catastrophe deductible: 2% of the insured value** (minimum $2,000)
  applies when the claim arises from a declared catastrophe event.

## 4. Additional living expenses (ALE)

When a covered peril makes the home uninhabitable, we pay reasonable additional
living expenses — hotel accommodation and meals above normal living costs — for
up to 24 months, subject to a sublimit of 20% of the insured value. Submit
receipts within 60 days.

## 5. Vulnerable policyholder fast-path

Households that include an occupant who is elderly (70+), has a medical device
dependency, or includes children under 6 are eligible for the vulnerability
fast-path: triage priority is raised and an advance hardship payment of up to
$2,000 may be released before final assessment. A signed hardship declaration
form is required.

## 6. Claim conditions

The policyholder must: (a) report the claim within 30 days of the event;
(b) take reasonable steps to prevent further damage; (c) provide an inventory
of damaged items when contents exceed $5,000; (d) make the property available
for inspection.
